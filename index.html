Field note 01 / Sizing and fit

Fit first: compare measurements, then choose a size

For a sneaker size chart comparison, record foot length in centimeters and read the exact model’s chart. Compare shoe insole measurements only when the seller explains how they were taken.

A hoodie chest measurement guide is most useful beside a garment you already own: measure it flat and compare like with like. For a T-shirt shoulder width guide or jacket sleeve measurements, check both the measurement diagram and the stated fit.

Field note 02 / OrientDig QC photo guide

Photos answer specific questions

To understand warehouse inspection photos, begin with the exact product ID, color and size. Check sneaker outsole photos for visible construction details and match sneaker color variants to the selected option.

For accessories, inspect clasp photos, stitching and attachment points. Photos can reveal visible differences, but they do not independently prove authenticity, material composition or long-term durability.

Field note 03 / OrientDig clothing spreadsheet

Build outfits around fit and fabric

Compare sweater fabric composition before choosing a heavier layer. For a pants waist and rise comparison, note whether the waistband measurement is flat, doubled or stretched. A shorts inseam measurement guide helps distinguish silhouettes that can look similar in cropped photographs.

When comparing matching outfit sets, check the top and bottom separately. Jersey fit comparison also needs a clear reference: a close athletic cut and a relaxed everyday fit can have very different measurements under the same size label.

Field note 04 / Smaller details

Accessories need measurements too

A baseball cap circumference guide should distinguish the adjustable range from a fixed size. For a belt length comparison, check whether the measurement includes the buckle and where the usable holes sit.

An underwear material and sizing guide should explain fabric composition, stretch and measurement method. Check the current seller’s return conditions before ordering items with fit or hygiene restrictions.

Field note 05 / Budget and availability

A listing amount is only one part of the budget

To estimate total international order cost, record the item amount and currency, domestic delivery, service charges, international freight and destination-specific taxes where applicable. Keep estimates separate from confirmed charges.

Recheck unavailable product links before making a replacement decision, and compare the exact product variants rather than similarly styled photographs. This page does not quote current fees, shipping rates or delivery dates.

How do I use an OrientDig spreadsheet link?

Open the original listing, confirm the product ID and selected size or color, and record the current seller details. If you intend to order through OrientDig, check its current link support and purchasing terms separately before submitting the item.

Do QC photos prove that an item is authentic?

No. Quality-control photos can help you assess visible details of the photographed item. They do not independently prove brand authorization, authenticity, material composition or long-term performance.

Which size should I choose?

Use the exact listing’s measurement chart and compare it with your body measurements or a well-fitting item you own. Check units, measuring method and fit notes instead of relying on an S, M or L label alone.

Are the pictured items available to buy here?

The photographs are visual references. They are not confirmed stock listings or offers. Check the destination website for the actual item, seller, availability and terms.

Can this guide quote shipping costs or delivery dates?

No. Shipping costs and delivery estimates depend on the destination, parcel measurements, selected service and current conditions. Request a current quote and review route restrictions before paying.
